PAKISTAN

Introduction

Pakistan is a country located in South Asia. It became independent on 14th August 1947. The capital city of Pakistan is Islamabad. Pakistan is known for its beautiful mountains, rivers, historical places and diverse culture.

Geography of Pakistan

Pakistan has a variety of geographical features including mountains, plains, deserts and coastal areas. The Karakoram mountain range is located in the northern part of the country. Pakistan is also home to 2 major rivers and many beautiful valleys.

National Information

National Language: Urdu
Capital: Islamabad
Currency: Pakistani Rupee
Independence Day: 14th August
